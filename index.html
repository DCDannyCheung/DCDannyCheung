Axis rotation and coordinate transformation

B = Rz(Ω) Rx(i) Rz(ω)
rPQW = Bᵀ rECI
Show matrix and sign conventions

R denotes a right-handed active rotation matrix; vectors are columns. The columns of B are the P, Q, and W directions expressed in ECI. The animation rotates about successive local axes by Ω, i, and ω. Coordinate conversion uses the inverse: Bᵀ = Rz(−ω) Rx(−i) Rz(−Ω). The target ECI → PQW matrix is:

[ -0.692 0.354 0.629 ] [ 0.418 -0.514 0.749 ] [ 0.589 0.781 0.208 ]

P points toward periapsis. Q is perpendicular to P in the orbital plane, in the direction of increasing true anomaly, and W = P × Q. The three angular elements Ω, i, ω fix the orbit's orientation; a and e fix its shape; ν fixes the position of r on that orbit. This animation demonstrates a change of basis, not satellite motion along the orbit.
